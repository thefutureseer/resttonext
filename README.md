# Spiritual Social Media Platform

Welcome to (my app with many working names such as) **Serene Seek / Monks on Demand / HUSH**, a unique social media platform designed to connect seekers, thinkers, and spiritual explorers. This space allows users to engage in meaningful discussions, share insights, and reflect on wisdom from both contemporary and historical spiritual masters.

## Features
- **Spiritual Timeline** – Follow the teachings of past masters and contribute to the evolving conversation.
- **Hashtag Filtering** – Discover content based on themes like #Mindfulness, #Meditation, #Philosophy, and more.
- **Community Engagement** – Comment, like, and share posts that resonate with your journey.
- **Personal Reflections** – Post your thoughts and experiences to inspire others.
- **Search & Explore** – Find wisdom by topic, author, or historical figure.
- **Authentication & Security** – Secure login and user-friendly authentication.

## 🏗️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL
- **Authentication:** JWT-based authentication
- **Deployment:** Vercel (frontend), Render.com (backend)
- **Package Manager:** pnpm
